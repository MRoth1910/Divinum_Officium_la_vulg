\mycentering{music.}
The men's schola, an all-volunteer group seeking to worship God as he desires, sings the chant for Sunday and feast-day Masses, Vespers, and other services, augmented by the Cantores Sanctæ Mariæ Magdalenæ, composed of ladies of the parish, other volunteers, and a professional core of singers who desire to make more widely known the church's treasury of sacred music, ornamenting the sacred liturgy with polyphonic and choral music, especially that of the period ca. 1450--1650.
